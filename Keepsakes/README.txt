Keepsakes 0.2.0

Install
1. Quit the game completely (a /reload does not pick up a new addon).
2. Unzip Keepsakes.zip into your AddOns folder, so you end up with
   World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Keepsakes\Keepsakes.toc
   (use the folder for your game version if it's named differently).
3. Start the game and make sure Keepsakes is ticked in the AddOns list.
   You should see "Keepsakes: loaded" in chat when you log in.

Use
- Alt-click an item in your bags to star it. A small star appears on it and its
  tooltip says "Keepsake". Alt-click again to unstar it.
- When you star an item, a box asks why you're keeping it ("first sword,
  Westfall"). Type a line and press Enter, or leave it empty. The line shows in
  the item's tooltip under "Keepsake".
- Alt-Shift-click a keepsake to write or change its story, or use
  /keep story <item>. Unstarring an item forgets its story.
- At a vendor, starred items get a faint gold tint. Clicking one asks
  "Sell it anyway?". Say yes and right-click it again to sell it.
- If you try to delete a starred item, it goes back in your bag and chat tells
  you to unstar it first.
- Stars are per item type and shared by all your characters.
- /keep <item> stars or unstars an item (shift-click a link into the command).
- /keep list shows your keepsakes and their stories.

Not covered
- Blizzard's "Sell all junk" button at vendors. Don't star grey items you want to keep.
